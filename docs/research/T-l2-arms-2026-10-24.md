1. **D1's identical-class rule — SATISFIED, no numerics event.** `generated_text` is
   byte-identical across all 9 runs (`diff` clean, C0 reps included). The arm declined safely

   holds 256 of one layer) holds at model scale, matching the tiny-check result at `a8c7e3dc`.
2. **D2's TTFT band — NO REGRESSION; the improvement expectation is untestable here — record
   and park.** Median TTFT −2.318 s vs C0 (C0's own spread is 1.16 s; D1-r3's 148.8 shows the

   falsifier read this is the "wiring is orchestration-cheap at this residency" outcome: ship
   Stage A+B as alignment. The P1-class TTFT expectation (−2.5–5 s at this 2-chunk shape;

   run, which requires a residency lever — **Stage C's `COLI_VK_TIER_POOL_LAYER` is not in this
   tree**. The spec's own D2 (pool-placed decode) and P1 (pool + rows) arms are therefore
   unrunnable at `7063a502`; nothing was substituted for them.
3. **Prefetch guard — unchanged.** `prefetched 12563 (10535 used)` in all 9 runs, identical
   tier behaviour (the standing ≈12.6 k count; no collapse — moot while the arm stays off, and
   the live guard for the Stage C arm).
4. **No crashes, no stop conditions tripped.**

## 3. K1 — the kernel microbench (one-off, `bench/up2276/k1_moe_rows.c`)

`vkc_moe_rows(7,…)` (Stage B's grouped mx GEMMs) vs `vkc_moe(7,…)` (the generic per-pair

| 2048 (nl=8) | — (no number, see below) | 99135.1 µs/layer | — |

- **Route (b)'s trigger does NOT fire**: the bar was the per-pair generic path >1.5× slower
  than the grouped path at S=1; measured 0.22× — the per-pair path is ~4.5× *faster* at decode
  shape. No new fused fmt-7 kernel is wanted; Stage A's split (per-pair ≤ 8 rows, grouped for
  chunks) is the right shape, and Stage B's throughput number is the 47.7/99.1 ms a
  layer-visit at 512/2048 rows (the crossover sits between S=128 and S=512).
- Cross-check against the spec §3 pool pricing: device-resident compute for a whole
  layer-visit at 2048 rows is ~0.1 s, so a pool-placed layer's visit stays PCIe-bandwidth-bound
  (3.75 GiB / 8.2 GB/s ≈ 0.46 s floor) — the spec's 0.5–0.9 s/visit band remains plausible;
  nothing here re-prices it.
- **A rig boundary, recorded not debugged:** single frames of ~2–4+ s of GPU work (48 heavy
  layer-calls at S ≥ 256–512, either path, and once surfacing at the next submit after heavy
  frames) trigger an amdgpu hard recovery ("The CS has been cancelled because the context is
  lost... guilty of a hard recovery", then `queue submit failed (-4)`). It is not a
  deterministic per-call fault: the same S=512 frame-load passes at 8 layers a frame, and the
  loss point varies run to run (`k1-rows-512-nl48-hang.log` vs the earlier isolations). The
  engine never builds frames of this shape (an armed layer adds one ~0.1 s call per chunk
  visit), the unit gate (S=40/S=7) and the tiny-check are unaffected, and the arms above ran
  clean. Microbenches on this rig should cap a frame's layer-calls at these shapes.
